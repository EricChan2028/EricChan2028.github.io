Research

How can a language model improve from its own experience without fooling itself?

Agents increasingly train on data they generate, judge and replay themselves. Each of those loops has a place where the training signal can drift away from what we actually want. My work finds those places, measures them, and fixes them with methods that come with guarantees and honest negative results.

01

Stable off-policy agentic RL

Reused KV caches make the sampler a hidden mix of old and new weights. I bound that bias and recompute only where the bound fails.

GCD · NeurIPS 2026 →
02

Diversity & constraints

Search archives of multi-agent designs instead of one winner, and optimize what survives deployment constraints rather than raw reward.

QD for multi-agent structures · under review → DeltaADMET-R1 · ICLR 2027 →
03

Self-improvement that stays honest

Measure how self-play curricula drift toward ill-posed problems, and credit each agent by the progress it causes, not the outcomes it can game.

Uncertainty ≠ QC · ACL 2027 → ProSPER · AAMAS 2027 →
